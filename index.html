Fare rules

Pontage (bridge) charge — Dec 2025

Fares rose on 20 Dec 2025 when the pontage (bridge-crossing) charge changed. All fares in this app are the post-change fares as reported in Dec 2025.

VAT

15% VAT is included in AC fares (Snigdha, AC Seat, AC Berth, AC Chair). Non-AC fares carry no VAT.

Online booking fee

Booking on eticket.railway.gov.bd adds 20 Tk per paying seat (adults and children aged 3–9). Children under 3 travel free and pay no fee.

Children

Children under 3 travel free. Children aged 3–9 pay half the adult fare. Children aged 10 and above pay the full adult fare.

When to book

Tickets open 10 days ahead at 8:00 AM on eticket.railway.gov.bd and at station counters.

Always verify

Fares can change. Verify the final fare on eticket.railway.gov.bd before paying. This app is not affiliated with Bangladesh Railway.

Sources & accuracy

  • Fares as reported Dec 2025 (post-pontage change). Every route source is listed in DATA_NOTES.md, including conflicts.
  • Rangpur–Dhaka fares are labelled ESTIMATED by their source. Feni–Dhaka rests on a single source. The Cox's Bazar Snigdha fare is reported as 1443, 1449 and 1450 by different sources.
  • No First Berth fare is verified for any route, so First Berth never appears in the fare table or calculator.
